# raster reproject: cubic + cubicspline kernels (4-sample, scaled,
# masked, nearest-substituted identity)
# Known accepted residuals (4 cases): masked scaled-cubic validity at
# strong downscales (bilinear-fallback finds samples where the

# into the spline kernel even for near-1:1 warps).
